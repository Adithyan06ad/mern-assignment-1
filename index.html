Artist: Vincent van Gogh • Location: The MoMA, New York

Painted from the east-facing window of Van Gogh's asylum room in Saint-Rémy-de-Provence, just before sunrise. The expressive swirling sky, radiant celestial bodies, and towering cypress tree capture nature’s transcendent vitality.
