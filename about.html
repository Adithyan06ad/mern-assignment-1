Curatorial Stewardship

Every artwork featured within Lumière is preserved in digital fidelity with exhaustive provenance records, pigment analysis, and cross-cultural historical context.

🔍

Scholarly Research

Our scholars collaborate with the Musée du Louvre, the Mauritshuis, and the MoMA to shed new light on master techniques like Vermeer's camera obscura and Leonardo's sfumato.

🌐

Global Open Access

Fine art belongs to humanity. Through our web exhibition platform, learners, students, and connoisseurs worldwide can explore historic art pieces without barriers.
